# Common federal return

Use this module for every return. Verify each procedural step against the exact-year Form 1040 and relevant schedule instructions.

## Normalize and reconcile

Create one record per source form and retain file, payer, form, box or row, amount, and confidence/provenance. Reconcile:

- W-2 wages, federal withholding, state wages, state withholding, and coded benefits across all employers.
- Interest and dividends across Forms 1099-INT and 1099-DIV, including tax-exempt interest and foreign-tax fields.
- Brokerage proceeds, basis, adjustments, and withholding to Form 1099-B summaries and lot detail.
- Other income, adjustments, credits, payments, and prior-year carryovers to their supporting form or worksheet.

Never treat a missing form, blank box, failed extraction, or unresolved discrepancy as zero.

## Choose the filing path

1. Establish filing status and dependent eligibility from facts, not labels on uploaded forms.
2. Determine which income, adjustment, deduction, credit, tax, and payment schedules are activated.
3. Compare the standard deduction with a supported itemized-deduction calculation. Do not assume itemizing merely because Forms 1098 or property-tax records exist.
4. Follow the exact-year tax-computation path selected by the Form 1040 instructions. The Tax Table, Tax Computation Worksheet, Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet, Schedule D Tax Worksheet, or another method are not interchangeable.
5. Map each result to the draft form only after its supporting worksheet reconciles.

## Simple-case gates

### Mortgage interest

Before using the bundled simple calculator, confirm its eligibility booleans from source records. At minimum, resolve the number of loans, acquisition and payoff/refinance dates, beginning and ending principal, additional borrowing, large principal prepayments, debt use, and any grandfathered or mixed-use debt. Obtain every Form 1098 and closing/refinance statement needed by the exact-year Publication 936 method.

If any required fact is unknown, more than one debt category applies, or the simple average-balance method is not permitted, stop that calculator path and complete the applicable Publication 936 worksheet.

### State or local income-tax refund

Do not include a Form 1099-G refund automatically. Obtain the corresponding prior-year return and determine whether the taxpayer itemized, deducted income tax rather than sales tax, received a tax benefit, was limited by the itemized-deduction or SALT rules, and had any other fact required by the exact-year refund worksheet or recovery rules.

Use the bundled calculator only when its supported-case checks pass and all required prior-year inputs are known. Otherwise complete the exact-year State and Local Income Tax Refund Worksheet or the recovery method directed by the Form 1040 instructions.

## Final cross-check

Reconcile withholding and estimated payments independently, preserve all source-to-line mappings, and present unresolved items separately from calculated amounts. Do not call an income-tax subtotal “total tax” when additional taxes or credits have not yet been evaluated.
