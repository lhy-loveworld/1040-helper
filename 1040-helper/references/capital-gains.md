# Capital gains and crypto

Use this module for Form 1099-B, broker statements, property dispositions, or digital-asset disposals.

## Brokerage lots

Normalize each sale or approved summary category with:

- Description and quantity.
- Acquisition and disposition dates.
- Proceeds, reported basis, corrected basis, and fees.
- Covered/noncovered status, holding period, and adjustment code/amount.
- Wash-sale amount and the replacement lot affected, when known.
- Source file and link to any employee-equity compensation already included in wages.

Reconcile normalized proceeds and basis to the broker's category totals before drafting Form 8949 or Schedule D. Use summary reporting only when the exact-year instructions permit it. Preserve lot detail outside chat.

Activate the exact official worksheet for collectibles, qualified small business stock, installment sales, inherited or gifted basis, worthless securities, wash sales across accounts, prior-year capital-loss carryovers, unrecaptured gain, or other special categories. Do not force these through an ordinary stock-sale path.

## Digital assets

Ask only after a document or intake answer indicates activity. Distinguish holdings from disposals. A sale, exchange, token-for-token swap, spend, or other transfer may need disposition analysis; rewards, staking, mining, airdrops, compensation, gifts, and transfers between the user's own wallets require different evidence paths.

Build a transaction ledger containing timestamp, asset and quantity sent/received, proceeds or value source, fees, acquisition lot, basis method, holding period, transaction hash/account label, and source record. Reconcile wallet and exchange transfers so they are not treated as taxable disposals twice.

Do not invent basis or fair market value. If transaction history is incomplete, wallets cannot be reconciled, or the selected lot method lacks support, report the affected amounts as unresolved and recommend specialist review before filing.

## Tax computation

After Schedule D is complete, follow the exact-year Form 1040 instructions to select the correct capital-gain tax worksheet. Do not calculate preferential tax independently from ordinary taxable income; the rates stack and the required worksheet may incorporate other gain categories.
