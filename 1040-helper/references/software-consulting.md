# Software and service consulting

Use this module for freelance development, advisory work, open-source sponsorship, app or SaaS revenue, 1099-NEC/1099-K receipts, or other self-employment income. Create one [Schedule C intake](../assets/schedule_c_template.md) per distinct trade or business.

## Default lane

Use the streamlined lane only for a cash-basis sole proprietor providing services with no employees, inventory, complex depreciation, entity return, or unresolved foreign reporting. A missing 1099 does not exclude income.

Reconcile books, bank or payment-processor records, Forms 1099-NEC/1099-K, cash receipts, refunds, and duplicated processor reporting. Separate personal transfers and reimbursements using records; do not net an unexplained difference.

## Expenses

Classify expenses under the exact-year Schedule C instructions and retain business purpose. Common engineer categories include cloud hosting, developer tools, software subscriptions, domains, payment-processing fees, office supplies, professional services, and continuing education related to the existing business.

For mixed-use phone, internet, subscriptions, travel, vehicles, or equipment, require a documented business-use allocation. Do not deduct personal costs, commuting, reimbursed costs, self-employed health insurance as an ordinary Schedule C insurance expense, or startup/education costs under a convenient category without checking their separate rules.

Route computers, equipment, purchased software, vehicles, and other longer-lived assets through the exact-year depreciation/expensing rules. Use the default lane only when the applicable treatment can be established from complete placed-in-service, cost, and business-use records.

## Home office

Confirm regular and exclusive use plus the other exact-year eligibility requirements before either method. Ask explicitly whether the same area was used for W-2 employee work, guests, recreation, or another activity; do not assume a dedicated-looking room satisfies the Schedule C requirements.

- **Simplified method:** Apply the exact-year simplified-method worksheet and limitations. Do not allocate otherwise deductible mortgage interest or real-estate taxes away from Schedule A and do not also deduct actual home expenses on Schedule C/Form 8829.
- **Actual-expense method:** Complete Form 8829 using direct and indirect expenses, business-use allocation, depreciation basis, and carryovers. Coordinate the amounts directed to Schedule A under the exact-year instructions.

For a user who wants the low-data path, evaluate the simplified method first without claiming it produces the largest deduction. Present a comparison only when the inputs support both and the user wants it.

## Downstream work

After Schedule C, evaluate self-employment tax, deductible self-employment-tax adjustments, self-employed health insurance, retirement-plan limits, estimated payments, and the qualified business income workflow under their exact-year instructions. Reconcile W-2 wages and workplace retirement contributions when they affect those calculations.

For an owner-only 401(k), use [solo-401k.md](solo-401k.md). Keep the proprietor's own-plan deduction separate from employee benefits on Schedule C, and follow its downstream effects through adjustments and QBI. Do not re-add a previously reported expense when the user repeats or clarifies its annual total.

Escalate payroll, inventory/cost of goods sold, multiple-owner entities, unclear worker classification, hobby-loss questions, substantial assets, or material carryovers.
