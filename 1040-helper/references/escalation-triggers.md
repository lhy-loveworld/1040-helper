# Escalation triggers

Use this checklist after document inventory and whenever new facts appear. A trigger does not require abandoning the entire return: isolate the affected calculation, name the missing facts or form, and continue unaffected work when safe.

## Route beyond the default lane

- Form 1040-NR, dual-status, treaty positions, expatriation, or uncertain U.S. tax residency.
- Foreign accounts, foreign trusts/entities, controlled foreign corporations, foreign pensions, foreign earned income, or foreign tax reporting beyond an uncomplicated credit.
- Schedule K-1, partnership/S corporation/entity returns, trusts or estates.
- Rental real estate, royalties with complex basis, passive-activity limitations, farms, natural resources, or multiple-owner businesses.
- Schedule C inventory, payroll/employees, uncertain worker classification, large asset dispositions, complex depreciation, casualty losses, or material carryovers.
- Missing or irreconcilable basis, wash sales across accounts, options/short sales, section-specific securities, collectibles, installment sales, or digital-asset history that cannot be reconstructed.
- ISO exercises or other facts activating AMT when AMT basis/credit records are unavailable.
- Net operating losses, passive losses, at-risk limitations, credit carryovers, recapture, amended-return interactions, or prior-year numbers that cannot be substantiated.
- Bankruptcy, insolvency, debt cancellation, foreclosure, divorce/property transfers, adoption, household employment, unusual dependent conflicts, or identity inconsistencies.
- A state residency, sourcing, or remote-work position that remains uncertain after reviewing official state instructions.

## Escalation behavior

1. Stop only the unsupported calculation and mark its dependent draft lines unresolved.
2. Identify the exact official form, worksheet, publication, or state instructions that govern it.
3. Ask the smallest set of questions or request the smallest set of records needed to determine whether the issue can be handled reproducibly.
4. Continue if the official line-by-line method is clear, inputs are complete, and the result can be audited.
5. Recommend an enrolled agent, CPA, or tax attorney when facts, elections, records, or legal interpretation remain material and unresolved.

Never replace an escalation with zero, a rough estimate, a generic web answer, or a calculator result outside its declared scope.
